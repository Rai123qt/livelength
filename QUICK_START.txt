LIVELENGTH MANAGER 2027 V3.9.9 - HUONG DAN NHANH

TU MA NGUON
1. Cai AutoCAD 2027 va .NET 10 SDK.
2. Chay BUILD.cmd de tao DLL. Script tu tim AutoCAD 2027.
3. Dong AutoCAD, chay BUILD_AND_INSTALL.cmd de build va cai plugin.
4. Mo AutoCAD 2027, go LLMGR.

TU BO CAI ZIP PHAT HANH
1. Dong AutoCAD 2027.
2. Giai nen ZIP, chay INSTALL_PORTABLE.cmd.
3. Mo AutoCAD 2027, go LLMGR.

Trong bang LLMGR, tab Chieu dai tao va quan ly MLeader co L=.
Tab Giat cap GC dang ky DIM/LINE/PLINE/ARC/SPLINE/ELLIPSE/MLINE va tao ghi chu GC.
Ghi chu co L= duoc cap nhat khi nguon thay doi tren may da cai plugin.
